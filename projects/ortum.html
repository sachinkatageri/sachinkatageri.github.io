Product story

Athletes perform at their best when they get the best coaching, the coaching that matches them best and brings out the best in them. Welcome to Ortum.

Ortum is an always-available excellence coach, built to meet every team member exactly where they are at.

What Ortum does

Ortum is trained to help team members understand what they need to do and show the best sequence and processes to use to complete the work.

It trains, coaches and helps answer questions and process-improvement ideas.

Experience focus

24/7 availability

Design opportunity

The product experience centers on turning coaching into actionable guidance: help the user understand the task, see the recommended sequence, ask questions when blocked, and continue with confidence.

Problem statement

Team members needed immediate, personalized guidance that could explain what to do, how to do it and what to improve during work.

Users / personas

Team Member

Needs immediate coaching, clear next steps and answers while doing the work.

Manager / Coach

Needs scalable guidance and consistent process support across teams.

Information architecture

The experience is centered on a conversational guidance loop: Ask → Understand context → Recommend → Practice → Improve. The assistant acts as an entry point to coaching rather than another complex navigation layer.

Key user flows

Get guidance

Question → Context → Recommended process → Next action.

Improve process

Ask → Explore idea → Coaching → Apply → Reflect.

Design system

The system emphasizes conversational states, recommendation cards, progress cues, coaching prompts and mobile-friendly interaction patterns so guidance remains actionable rather than becoming a long chat transcript.

Validation

Validation centered on whether coaching responses could be understood and acted on quickly. The experience should make the next step obvious, preserve user control and support recovery when the recommendation is not useful.

End metrics / impact

No verified post-launch metrics were supplied for this case study. The portfolio therefore focuses on the product problem, experience model, design decisions and validation approach rather than inventing impact numbers.
