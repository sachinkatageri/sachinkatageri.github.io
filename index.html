HOW I WORK

From discovery to shipped product.

The visual changes. The process stays grounded in understanding the problem, testing assumptions and making decisions visible.

01

Discover

Clarify the business goal, user need, constraints and success signals.

02

Define

Map journeys, flows, information architecture and the highest-value problems.

03

Design

Explore concepts, prototype interactions and build a coherent visual language.

04

Validate

Use feedback, usability checks and stakeholder review to remove uncertainty.

05

Ship & learn

Support handoff, refine details and keep improving the experience after release.
