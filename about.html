The story

Engineering gave me the logic. Design gave me the creativity.

I’m Sachin Katageri, a UI UX Product Designer with 7+ years of experience designing B2B SaaS, AI-driven products, enterprise platforms, and web and mobile experiences.

I started with mechanical engineering, moved into graphic design, and then into UI/UX. That path taught me to connect systems thinking with visual communication and user needs.

Today I work across research, interaction design, visual design, design systems and product thinking to create practical experiences that teams can build and users can understand.
